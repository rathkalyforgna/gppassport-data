# GP Passport — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** December 1, 2026

GP Passport ("the app") is developed by Rathkalyforgna Chhin ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

**The short version:** we don't run servers that collect your data, we don't use analytics or advertising tools, and we don't track you. Your passport and profile are stored on your device and in your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

---

## 1. Information you add to the app

You choose what to enter. It stays in your control:

| Data | Examples | Where it's stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile | Name, country, optional profile photo | Your device and your private iCloud database |
| Races you attended | Grand Prix, season, date, ticket type, seat, who you went with, notes, podium, weather | Your device and your private iCloud database |
| App preferences | Onboarding completed, races you follow for ticket alerts | Your device only |

**iCloud sync.** If you're signed in to iCloud, the app uses Apple's CloudKit to sync this data between your devices through your **private** iCloud database. That data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We **cannot see, access or export it**. If you're not signed in to iCloud, your data stays on your device.

**Other people.** If you write other people's names in "Who you went with" or in your notes, that text is stored the same way and is never sent to us. Please only add information you have the right to record.

## 2. Photos

- **Profile photo:** if you pick one with the system photo picker, the app receives **only the photo you choose**, not your library. It's stored with your profile as described above.
- **Saving share cards:** if you tap Photos when sharing, the app asks for **add-only** access so it can save the image you made. It can't read your library.

## 3. Sharing

When you share a passport or stamp card, you choose where it goes: Instagram Stories, Photos, or any app in the iOS share sheet. The image is handed to the app you pick, and its own privacy policy then applies. Nothing is sent to us.

## 4. Notifications

Pro ticket alerts are **local** notifications scheduled on your device for ticket sales on races you follow. To keep them up to date, the app may download the latest ticket dates in the background, as described below. Which races you follow stays on your device. No push-notification tokens or personal data are sent to us.

## 5. Subscriptions and purchases

GP Passport Pro and the Race Weekend Pass are sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app reads your purchase history **on your device**, through Apple's StoreKit, to unlock Pro and to work out which foil stamps you've earned. That history isn't sent to us.

## 6. Services the app connects to

The app contacts the services below to work. Like any internet request, these include your device's IP address and standard technical information. **They don't include your name, your passport or anything else you've entered.**

| Service | Purpose | What is sent | Policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jolpica F1 API (api.jolpi.ca) | Race calendars, session times, season history and driver lists | The requested season or circuit | [jolpi.ca](https://jolpi.ca) |
| GP Passport ticket data (rathkalyforgna.github.io) | Downloading the latest Grand Prix ticket dates and prices | A standard file request, with no personal data | [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) |
| Apple Maps (MapKit) | Showing circuit maps | The circuit's location | [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) |
| Apple App Store (StoreKit) | Pro purchases and restoring them | Handled entirely by Apple | [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) |
| Apple iCloud (CloudKit) | Syncing your data between your devices | Your app data, in your private database | [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) |

The app does **not** ask for your location. Maps show circuit locations, not where you are.

## 7. What we don't do

- We don't collect, sell, rent or share personal information.
- We don't use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
- We don't track you across apps or websites (no advertising identifier, no tracking prompt).
- We don't run user accounts or keep your data on our own servers.

If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics. We use them only to improve the app.

## 8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it.

- **Delete a visit or your profile photo** in the app.
- **Delete everything on a device:** delete the app.
- **Delete synced iCloud data:** on iPhone go to **Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › GP Passport › Delete Data**.

Because we never hold your data, there's nothing on our side to delete. We're still happy to help if you contact us.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data. You can do all of these yourself in the app and in your iCloud settings, because the data stays under your control. For anything else, contact us below.

## 10. Children

GP Passport is a general-audience app and isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and we don't collect personal information from anyone on our own servers.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes, for example if a future version adds new features that need different data. We'll post the new version at this address with a new effective date and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
