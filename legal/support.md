## Getting started

**How do I log a race?**
Tap **Log a Race** in Passport. Choose the Grand Prix and the season you went, and the date is set to that race weekend. Each Grand Prix can be logged once per season, and each visit earns its stamp.

**Why can't I pick a certain season or date?**
GP Passport checks every visit against the official race calendar, so you can only choose seasons when that Grand Prix was held and days of its race weekend. A race that hasn't happened yet can be logged once you've been.

**Can I edit or delete a visit?**
Yes. Open the race in Passport and tap the visit to edit it. **Delete Visit** is at the bottom of that screen, or swipe left on the visit.

## GP Passport Pro

**What does Pro include?**
Foil stamps (gold and neon for some races), premium share themes (Midnight, Carbon Gold and Racing Red) and ticket sale alerts. Everything in the free app stays free.

**What's the Race Weekend Pass?**
A one-time purchase that gives you 7 days of Pro. It doesn't renew and you're never charged again. Buying another while one is running adds 7 more days after it ends.

**Do I keep foil stamps if Pro ends?**
Stamps for races you attended while you were Pro, or up to 14 days before you started, keep their foil for good. Other stamps go back to classic ink and come back in foil if you subscribe again.

**How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?**
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open **Settings › GP Passport Pro** in the app and tap **Restore Purchases**.

**What happens if I switch between Monthly and Annual?**
Monthly to Annual starts right away. You're charged the Annual price that day, and Apple refunds the unused part of your month. Annual to Monthly starts when your paid year ends, so you keep Annual until then. To switch, open the paywall in **Settings › GP Passport Pro** and tap **Switch to …**, or use **Manage Subscription**.

**How do I cancel or change my subscription?**
On your iPhone, open **Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › GP Passport**. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

**How do I get a refund?**
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Your data

**Where is my passport stored?**
On your iPhone and in your private iCloud account, synced across your devices when you're signed in to iCloud. We can't see or access it. See our [Privacy Policy](https://rathkalyforgna.github.io/gppassport-data/privacy.html).

**My passport is empty after reinstalling or changing iCloud settings.**
Your stamps and profile are safe in your iCloud. When iCloud is turned off for GP Passport, or you sign out of iCloud, your iPhone removes its synced copy, so the app looks empty. To bring everything back, open **Settings › [your name] › iCloud**, make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account, and turn on **GP Passport**. Then open the app while online. Your passport downloads automatically, which can take a few minutes. You can check progress in the app under **Settings › iCloud Sync**.

**My visits aren't syncing to my other device.**
Check you're signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on for GP Passport (**Settings › [your name] › iCloud**), then open the app on both devices while online. Syncing can take a few minutes.

## Ticket alerts

**I'm not getting ticket alerts.**
Ticket alerts are part of Pro. Follow a race on its ticket page, and make sure notifications are allowed in **Settings › Notifications › GP Passport**. Ticket dates come from public sources, so always confirm on the official site before buying.
